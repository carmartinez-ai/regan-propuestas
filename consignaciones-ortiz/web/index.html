About us

A family firm, on duty all year round

Our ship's agency was founded by Mr José María Ortiz A., General Manager, in September 1984. He had previously been OPS Manager of Camilo Martinón Navarro, S.L. during Las Palmas' best period of fishing transhipments (1960–1984).

Since then we have grown as a family firm, personally assisting each customer with everything their ship requires.

  1. 1960–1984

    Operations experience during the peak of fishing transhipments in Las Palmas.

  2. 1984

    José María Ortiz A. founds Consignaciones Ortiz in Las Palmas.

  3. Today

    Offices in Las Palmas and Tenerife, customs at both airports and a 24-hour service.
